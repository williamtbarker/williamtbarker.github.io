William T. Barker, PhD

Scientist · Independent Consultant · Science-Fiction Author

I am a scientist, independent consultant, and science-fiction author working at the intersection of complex systems, evidence, and decision-making.

Through SignalForge Advisors, I help pharmaceutical, biotechnology, diagnostics, medtech, CRO, and life-sciences R&D teams turn wet-lab evidence, omics outputs, fragmented data, and emerging AI capabilities into decision-ready workflows. My work spans AI and data strategy, bioinformatics and omics, scientific diligence, workflow design, and controlled implementation of RAG and agentic systems.

My scientific background includes medicinal chemistry, antibiotic resistance, microbiology, influenza virology, vaccine R&D, reverse genetics, transcriptomics, and machine learning. Before founding SignalForge, I worked in biotechnology and vaccine development at Agile Sciences and CSL Seqirus.

I also write systems-driven science fiction about technology, institutions, belief, and the consequences of human decisions. My current novel portfolio includes SOFTMAX, The Orchard, and The Gospel According to Greg.
