About

Bench science, computation, and strategy.

I’m a PhD chemist and microbiologist whose work spans medicinal chemistry, antibiotic resistance, influenza virology, vaccine R&D, transcriptomics, machine learning, and scientific software.

Before founding SignalForge Advisors, I worked in biotechnology and vaccine development at Agile Sciences and CSL Seqirus. That background shapes how I build: scientific assumptions are documented, evaluation boundaries are explicit, and software tests are never presented as biological validation.
